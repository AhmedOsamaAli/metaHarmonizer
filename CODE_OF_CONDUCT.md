# Code of Conduct

MetaHarmonizer contributors and operators must maintain a respectful,
professional, and inclusive environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and respect.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect patient privacy, credentials, and unpublished research data.
- Give credit for ideas, code, review, and operational work.
- Accept constructive review and correct mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate privacy
violations, credential sharing, and publication of another person's private
information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or an institutional
maintainer. Reports will be reviewed confidentially, conflicts of interest will
be disclosed, and proportionate action may include warnings, removal of content,
revocation of access, or exclusion from project spaces.

This policy applies to repository activity, reviews, project communication,
operations channels, and project events.
